## Facts he has published (the only facts drafts may state about him)
- Founder and chief consultant, Swayambhuti Consultants, India.
- 20+ years of experience; 150+ businesses transformed; 300,000+ professionals impacted through consulting and training.
- Framework: the SME Performance Engine™ — Systemize (SOPs, KPIs, delegation), Humanize (leaders, culture,
  customer experience), Monetize (forecastable sales system, referrals, pricing discipline).
- Trains sales teams, especially in banks and financial services; runs campus-to-corporate programmes for MBA colleges.
- Offers a free 60-minute Business Performance Audit and a 3-minute online Business Engine Health Check (swayambhuti.com/health-check).

## Beliefs he repeats
- Most SME problems are system problems, not strategy or talent problems.
- The owner should not be the bottleneck; the business should run on documented processes and a weekly KPI rhythm.
- Sales results come from a repeatable process and coaching, not from hiring "better salespeople".

## Tone
- Direct, practical, warm. Speaks like an experienced trainer in the room, not a guru.
- Short sentences. One idea per paragraph. Concrete examples from Indian workplaces (branch, RM, MD, placement cell).
- Confident but not boastful. No hype, no hustle-culture language.
