## Key points

- Signs you are the bottleneck: approvals wait for you, the team calls you on holiday, nothing moves when you are travelling.
- Decision rights: list the decisions you make every week and assign each one an owner and a limit.
- Replace daily check-ins with a weekly KPI review.
- A 90-day plan to hand over the first five decision areas.
