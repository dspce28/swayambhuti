## Key points

- Underperformance is usually missing structure: no defined pipeline stages, no daily activity targets, no review rhythm.
- Hiring "better salespeople" into a broken system produces the same results.
- The three things to install first: a written sales process, a weekly pipeline review, and activity metrics that predict revenue.
- Example from bank and financial-services sales teams: what changes when every relationship manager follows the same call-to-close steps.
